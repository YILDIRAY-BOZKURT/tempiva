Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

SupportTürkçe

Tempiva is an appointment book that works on your device. We run no servers and there is no account. We do not collect, receive or sell your personal data.

  • Tempiva does not ask you to create an account.
  • The information you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to any other server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.
  • Your information never reaches us, so it cannot be sold or shared.

Data you enter

Appointments, customer names and phone numbers, notes, services, prices, payments, packages and your business details are stored in the app’s database on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

Device features

  • Notifications: reminders and the morning summary are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Face ID and Touch ID: app lock is optional. iOS performs the check and tells the app only the result. The app never receives biometric data.
  • Contacts: when you choose someone from your contacts, iOS shows its own picker. The app receives only the contact you pick, never your full address book.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it shares no data with third parties. Some actions open another app at your request: Call opens the Phone app, and WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with a message you can review before sending. What happens next is governed by that app’s privacy policy.

Backups and exports

Backup and CSV files are created only when you ask for them, and they go wherever you choose. Keeping those files safe is your responsibility. If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, iOS may include the app’s data in your device backup, according to your own Apple account settings.

Apple

Apple handles App Store downloads. Apple may give developers aggregated statistics and crash reports from users who agreed to share them. These do not contain the records you enter in the app.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records in the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Files you saved or shared must be deleted separately. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for you to request from us. You can withdraw any permission in iOS Settings at any time.

Your customers’ information

If you record information about your customers, you decide what is stored and why, and you are responsible for following the privacy laws that apply to your business, such as the GDPR or KVKK. Keep only what you need, and avoid sensitive details unless you have a valid reason.

Children

Tempiva is made for business use and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
