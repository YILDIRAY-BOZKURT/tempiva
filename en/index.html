How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings › Create backup in Tempiva and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Install Tempiva on the new phone, then choose Settings › Restore from backup. Restoring replaces any data already on that phone.

Why are my reminders not arriving?

In Tempiva, check that Settings › Appointment notification is on. Then make sure notifications are allowed for Tempiva in iOS Settings.

Why does the WhatsApp button not work?

WhatsApp must be installed on your phone, and the customer needs a saved phone number. Tempiva opens WhatsApp with the message ready, and you send it yourself.

Face ID does not unlock the app. What can I do?

App lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. If Face ID or Touch ID fails, enter your device passcode.

Can you recover my data?

No. Your data exists only on your phone and in backups you made, so we cannot restore it. Please make regular backups.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records in the app, or delete Tempiva from your iPhone to remove everything. Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted separately.
